Please trace code and durable authority, not only the happy path.

1. Can one physical file rewrite/truncate/generation be interpreted as a newer logical observation when it is actually older?
2. Can the same source observation create duplicate work, duplicate episode births, or a second terminal transition?
3. Can a source lease/admission failure be hidden while downstream Probability work still executes?
4. Can scheduler coalescing, same-key serialization, protected reserve or revision supersession starve a runnable decision task?
5. Can Probability IPC bind a result to a different bar, episode, parent-resolution authority, or physical revision than the request?
6. Can a late Probability result overwrite a newer or terminal fact?
7. Can restart/high-water recovery resurrect a terminal episode or repeat R1/Seal work?
8. Can durable terminal truth and UI projection diverge under crashes, SQLite stalls, queue saturation or fsync delay?
9. Can a radar/secondary lane mutate hidden lifecycle/model state before stale-epoch rejection?
10. Do Saudi/US/FX paths share contracts where they should, and are market-specific authorities explicit where they must differ?
11. Look for locks spanning SQLite/fsync/durable I/O, unbounded queues, synchronous fallback on hot workers, and retry amplification.
12. Identify every exception converted to continue/skip/fallback and ask whether truth, durable debt and observability are preserved.
13. For F32, prove that US Probability work cannot bypass or silently downgrade parent resolution authority.
14. For F33, prove that the US authority repair does not break inherited non-US Probability IPC call signatures.

For every suspected defect classify it as:

`OBSERVATION -> HYPOTHESIS -> CAUSAL PROOF -> IMPACT -> MINIMAL FIX -> REGRESSION TEST`

Do not promote a fixture assumption to a production finding without proving the same precondition in real production state.
